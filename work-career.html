Work & Career Counseling in Towson, Maryland

Work takes most of your waking hours. When it is hurting, everything hurts. Whether you are burned out, stuck under a difficult boss, or standing at a crossroads, counseling gives you room to sort out what work is doing to you and what you want to do about it.

When work follows you home

The Sunday night dread that starts earlier every week. The inbox you check in bed. The boss whose name in your notifications makes your stomach drop. Or the quieter version: you are good at your job, maybe even successful, and somewhere along the way it stopped meaning anything. You are running on fumes and calling it normal.

Work problems are rarely just work problems. A career carries identity, security, pride, and fear all at once, which is why a bad season at work can unsettle everything else. It is also why a difficult decision, whether to stay, push back, or leave, can feel impossible to make alone at 2 am. This is a place to make it with someone.

How work and career counseling helps

In our sessions at my Towson office, we will work together to:

  • Untangle burnout from wrong fit, because the remedy for each is different
  • Navigate difficult workplace dynamics, from an impossible manager to a culture that grinds you down
  • Face a crossroads with clarity: stay, change, or go, decided on purpose instead of by exhaustion
  • Rebuild confidence after a layoff, a failure, or a season of feeling like a fraud
  • Draw real boundaries between work and the rest of your life, and hold them

This is therapy, not resume coaching. We work on the person carrying the career, because when you change, your options tend to change with you.

Common Questions

Work and career counseling, answered.

Is this career coaching or therapy?

Therapy. A coach works on your resume and your next move; we work on the person making the move: the burnout, the confidence, the patterns that follow you from job to job. The two can complement each other.

My job really is the problem. Can therapy still help?

Yes. Even when the workplace is genuinely bad, you still face real decisions about how to respond, protect yourself, and plan your exit. Therapy helps you act from clarity instead of depletion.

Do you offer online therapy?

Yes. In addition to in-person sessions at the Towson office, I offer secure video sessions for clients located anywhere in Maryland. Many people mix the two, coming into the office when they can and meeting online when life gets busy.

Do you offer evening appointments?

Yes. Evening sessions are available Monday through Thursday until 8:00 pm.
